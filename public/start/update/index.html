Update Film Room

For people who already have Film Room. It takes about a minute, and your notes stay put.

Download Film Room v0.6d · 35 KB

Not installed yet? Start here instead.

  1. Unzip it

    Windows: right-click the downloaded file and choose Extract All. Mac: double-click it. You get a folder called film-room-overlay.

  2. Copy it over the old one

    Open the folder you originally loaded Film Room from (the one with manifest.json inside). Copy everything from the new film-room-overlay folder into it, and choose Replace when asked.

    Use the same folder. Your browser treats a different folder as a different extension, and your notes would not be there.

  3. Reload it

    Paste this into a new tab’s address bar and press Enter:

    brave://extensions

    On Chrome, use chrome://extensions instead.

    Find Film Room and click the circular reload arrow on its card.

  4. Reload YouTube

    Reload any YouTube tab that was already open (F5). The new version only runs on a freshly loaded page.

Check that it worked: open a YouTube video. The small “Film Room” tag at the top right of the video now says v0.6d. If it still shows an older version, redo steps 3 and 4.

What’s new

  • + Note button. The panel and the notes list now have a + Note button next to + Bookmark, the same as pressing Alt + N.
  • The panel is always there. It shows even before a video has any notes, so you can start from it.
  • Move a note while you edit it. Click the pencil next to a note in the list, then drag the note on the video to move it, or drag its corner to reshape it. Cancel puts it back as it was.
  • Longer notes stay longer. A note with more words stays on screen longer on its own (up to 12 seconds). The slider in the note box still overrides it.
