Keys

With a YouTube video open. On a Mac, Alt is the Option key. Keys don’t work while you’re typing in a text box.

Alt + NAdd a note at this moment (or click + Note in the panel). Type it, pick an emoji if you like, press Enter. Notes start about 2 seconds before you pressed, so the viewer sees it coming.
Alt + BQuick bookmark with one keypress. For the next 8 seconds, press 1 to 8 to change its emoji.
Alt + EEnd the note that is on screen right now.
Alt + PNotes panel in YouTube’s right-hand column: your list of notes and bookmarks. Click a row to jump there.
Alt + ,  Alt + .Previous / next note.
Alt + SShare your notes as a link.

What the emoji mean

  • 👀 Watch this
  • 🎯 Target or placement
  • 💡 Tip or idea
  • ✅ Good execution
  • ⭐ Great, copy this
  • ❌ Poor execution
  • ⚠️ Risky choice
  • 🤔 Why? Discuss this

On the video

  • Drag a note to move it. Drag its bottom-right corner to reshape it. Double-click your own note to edit it (or click the pencil that shows when you point at it). To put a moved note back, click the ↺ button on it.
  • The thin bar along the bottom of a note shows how much time it has left on screen. Longer notes stay up longer on their own; the slider in the note box changes it.
  • To fix a note, click the pencil next to it in the panel. While you edit, you can drag the note on the video to move it, or drag its corner to reshape it. Cancel puts it back the way it was.
  • Everything you write stays in your own browser, for that video. Nothing is uploaded until you share. It does not follow you to another computer, so use Export and Import to move it.

Drawing

  • Press Alt + A (or Draw on video in the panel). The video pauses and a bar appears on the picture: Arrow, Circle, Box, Erase, how long a shape stays, and Done. Drag on the picture to draw. Esc or Done goes back and the video carries on.
  • A shape shows for a few seconds from the moment you drew it, like a note. Pause where you want it, then draw. In the draw bar, the seconds button changes how long new shapes stay.
  • Pick Erase (or press 4) and click one of your shapes to remove it. Clear shapes in the panel removes all of yours on this video. Both have Undo for 8 seconds. Undo in the draw bar (or Ctrl + Z) takes back the last shape you drew, or brings back the last one you erased.
  • Shapes go into your share link along with your notes, and show on the viewer page. Shapes shared with you appear in blue and are read-only.

In the panel

  • Point at one of your own rows to get a pencil (edit) and an × (delete). Shared rows are read-only and carry a badge.
  • Deleted something by mistake? An Undo bar shows for 8 seconds. It covers single notes and Remove shared, which clears all shared notes from the video.
  • The bookmark row sets the emoji Alt + B uses by default and how many seconds early a note starts.
  • Export notes saves every note you have made, for all videos, to one file. Import notes adds them back on another computer or after a reinstall. Notes that are already there are skipped, and nothing is overwritten. Do an export now and then as a backup.

A good way to work

  • Watch the video once with Alt + B, dropping bookmarks. Go back afterwards and add words where it matters.
  • Keep notes to a line. One idea per note.

Sharing

Press Alt + S (or Copy link in the panel). Film Room copies a short link. Paste it into a text or email.

What the other person sees

  • They have Film Room: the link opens the video on YouTube with your notes drawn on top of it.
  • They don’t: it opens a simple page with the video and your notes beside it. They should watch it on that page. If they tap through to YouTube, they get the plain video without the notes.

Good to know

  • Anyone with the link can read the notes. Don’t put anything private in notes you share.
  • Sharing stores only the video, its title, your notes and a random ID that isn’t tied to you. No account, no email.
  • A link stops working after 90 days without anyone opening it. Add more notes, and sharing again makes a new link.
  • If you open your own link, Film Room tells you so and doesn’t duplicate your notes.

If something doesn’t work

  • Nothing shows up: reload the YouTube tab. Notes are hidden during ads, and Film Room only runs on youtube.com watch pages.
  • A key does nothing: make sure you’re not typing in a text box, and try clicking an empty part of the page first. If another extension uses the same key, it may win.
  • Updates: when I send a new zip, unzip it over the same folder, then click the reload arrow on Film Room in the extensions page.

Film Room is an early personal project. It runs only in your browser, and the share links are the only thing stored on a server.
