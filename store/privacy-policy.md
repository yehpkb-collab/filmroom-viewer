# Film Room Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Film Room is a browser extension that lets you add timed notes, bookmarks and labeled shapes to YouTube videos and share them by link.

## What stays on your device
Your notes, bookmarks, shapes, settings and your optional author name are saved in your browser's local extension storage. They are not sent anywhere unless you choose to share (see below). Backup files you save are created on your own computer.

## What leaves your device
Only when you press "Copy share link", Film Room sends the following to filmroom-viewer.netlify.app: the YouTube video id and title, the notes and emoji, the shapes and shape labels, and the author name if you set one. The service returns a short link. Anyone with the link can see that content.

Opening a share link makes the extension (or the viewer page) request that content from the same service.

## How long shared content is kept
A shared link is stored for 90 days after it was last opened. Opening it resets the clock. After that it is deleted automatically. Rate-limit counters (used to prevent abuse) are kept briefly and also deleted automatically; the service may see your IP address for that purpose.

## What Film Room does not do
- It does not collect your browsing history, YouTube account, or the videos you watch.
- It does not use analytics or tracking, show ads, or sell or share data with third parties.
- It does not run remote code.

## Hosting
The share service runs on Netlify. Netlify processes requests as our hosting provider under its own privacy policy.

## Permissions
- Storage: saves your notes and settings locally.
- Access to youtube.com: draws the notes and shapes over the video player.
- Access to filmroom-viewer.netlify.app: opens shared links in the extension.
