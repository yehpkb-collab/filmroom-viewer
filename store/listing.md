# Chrome Web Store listing (Film Room)

**Name:** Film Room
**Category:** Productivity (alt: Education)
**Visibility:** Unlisted
**Summary (max 132 chars):** Add timed notes and labeled drawings to YouTube videos, then share them with a link. No account needed.

**Description:**
Film Room turns any YouTube video into a review session.
- Press Alt+N to add a note at the current moment, or Alt+B to drop a quick bookmark without stopping the video.
- Press Alt+A to draw arrows, circles and boxes on the picture, and add a label to any shape.
- Notes and shapes appear at the right moment when the video plays. Alt+H hides them.
- Press Alt+S (or "Copy share link") to get a link. The person who opens it sees your notes and drawings, even without the extension.
- Everything is saved on your device. Back up or restore it from the panel. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Made for coaches, players, teachers and anyone who reviews video.

**Single purpose:** Add timed notes and drawings to YouTube videos and share them by link.

**Permission justifications:**
- storage: saves the user's notes, shapes and settings on their device.
- Host access youtube.com: draws notes and shapes over the video player and reads the player's current time.
- Host access filmroom-viewer.netlify.app: when the user opens a Film Room share link, the extension reads it and takes them to the video with the notes shown.

**Data use disclosure:** collects user-generated content (notes, shapes) only when the user presses Copy share link. Not sold, not used for anything else. Remote code: none.
